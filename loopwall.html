Loopwall commands

Run any line with Shift+Enter. Targets accept an index, "all", or an array like [0,2].

search("query", count)
Fetches clips and places them straight onto the wall, indexed in order. count defaults to 8.
play(target)
Plays the target(s). No argument plays everything.
pause(target)
Pauses the target(s). No argument pauses everything.
mute(target) / unmute(target)
Toggles sound per tile. New clips play unmuted by default.
seek(target, seconds)
Jumps to a timestamp.
loopAt(target, start, duration)
Loops a fixed window of the clip, from start for duration seconds.
loop(target, secondsAgo, duration)
Loops starting from secondsAgo before the clip's current position.
cue(target, "id")
Swaps in a new clip on an existing tile, same position on the wall.
phase(target, seconds)
Nudges playback position by seconds (+ forward, − back) — for pulling clips in or out of sync.
fadeOut(target)
Fades the tile to black over a second, then pauses it.
